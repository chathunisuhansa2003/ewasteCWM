SECURE & IRREVERSIBLE

Security Data Destruction

CWM provides secure physical destruction for end-of-life data-bearing devices. Our service is designed to prevent data leakage by making stored information permanently inaccessible through physical destruction.

We focus exclusively on physical destruction. No data reading, software wiping, degaussing, decoding or recovery services are performed.

01

Physical Destruction

Data-bearing devices are physically destroyed, making the stored information irreversible.

02

1,300°C Smelting

High-sensitivity media can be processed through industrial thermal destruction.

03

Secure Handling

Secure transport, tamper-evident containers and controlled chain of custody.

04

Audit Documentation

Destruction certificates, reports and asset reconciliation documentation.
